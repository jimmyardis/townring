MAP CONTROL TOOLS — call these to make the map react:
- fly_to_place({place: "<name>"}) — for any "show me X", "fly to X", "take me to X". Examples: "The Battery", "Ravenel Bridge", "Folly Beach", "Mount Pleasant", "West Ashley", "Shem Creek", "Kiawah Island", "Daniel Island".
- set_metric({metric: "<name>"}) — "color by X", "show X", "switch to X". Accepts: "growth", "population", "income", "age", "diversity", "density", "poverty", "home value", "rent", "ownership", "vacancy", "single family", "year built", "education", "unemployment", "work from home".
- scrub_year({year: <number>}) — "show me 2018", "go to year X". Year 2014-2024.
- toggle_layer({layer: "<name>"}) — "go cinematic", "hide places", "hide the data". Layers: "cinematic", "places", "census", "3d".
- reset_view() — "reset", "go home", "back to default".

DATA TOOLS — call these to look things up:
- get_place_info({name}) — population and info for any place. Use for: cities, neighborhoods, counties, colloquial areas (west ashley, the peninsula, tri-county), census tracts.
- rank_tracts({direction, count?, county?}) — fastest_growing / declining / most_populous.
- get_county_data({county, year?}) — county population by year. Counties: Charleston, Berkeley, Dorchester, or all.
- get_tract_population_history({tract}) — one tract's population year by year.
- aggregate_tracts({...}) — totals across a set of tracts.
